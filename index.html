Connect your headset

Plug your headset into the device you're using right now, then choose Connect headset and pick your Quest from the list.

HostThis device, the one showing this page ReceiverYour Quest headset
Supported: Quest 1, Quest 2, Quest 3, Quest 3S and Quest Pro. Other headsets aren't supported.
  • Turn on developer mode for your headset.
  • Use a USB cable that carries data, not a charge-only one.
  • Close other ADB tools on your computer, since they hold the USB connection.
  • Use Chrome or Edge on a computer. Firefox and Safari don't support WebUSB.

Waiting for the headset

Put the headset on and choose Allow on the USB debugging prompt. Tick Always allow to skip the prompt next time.

Compatible games

These are the IL2CPP Unity games installed on your headset. Patching itself isn't built yet; games marked Patched are ready for mods.

Test the patch agent

Copies a small test program to the headset and runs it. It only writes one temporary file.
